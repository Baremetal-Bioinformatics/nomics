Where nomics goes next

Six research briefs surveyed the major bioinformatics pipeline families: somatic, germline extensions, long reads, bulk RNA-seq, single-cell and epigenomics. Each one asked the same questions: can the reference be reproduced exactly, what can we reuse, what would it take, and who needs it. This page turns them into a proposal.

Plan, 2026-10-15 · priorities agreed · effort in engineer-weeks at the pace germline showed (about one agent-assisted week per pipeline stage) · briefs in docs/research/pipelines/

What nomics is for

Exactly the output of a named, current nf-core pipeline, at every stage boundary, on x86 and Arm, several times faster on one small box, with no licence. A lab validated on the reference switches by checking digests instead of revalidating.

NVIDIA ParabricksFree and GPU-accelerated, but "functionally equivalent" to GATK 4.3 and BWA 0.7.15, not identical to today's pins.
SentieonMatches GATK 3.7–4.4 by its own account; licensed per core-year. DNAscope sells better accuracy, not equivalence.
Illumina DRAGENFastest of all on FPGA and instruments; claims higher accuracy, and a 2025 benchmark found it the least concordant with GATK.
elPrep 5The only precedent for bit-exact output (GATK 4.1.3.0, Java mode), but with no aligner and no release since 2022.

Each new pipeline should keep that claim intact. So the ranking below favours work where the reference is deterministic and open, and where code we already trust carries over. It does not reward headline speed alone, where Parabricks and DRAGEN are hard to beat.

Decided, and still open

  1. Depth before breadth: decided, finish sarek first.GVCF mode, joint genotyping, the Mutect2 somatic chain, then TIDDIT structural variants. Every one keeps "identical to sarek" true for more of what sarek users run, and sarek work gets first call on the Spark.
  2. Next modality: decided, expression before long reads.Bulk RNA-seq and single-cell share one exact STAR, so they form one track. Long reads and DeepVariant move to later, which also defers the question of a non-exact gate for neural-network callers.
  3. Annotation: out of scope.nomics stops at the VCF and does not port VEP.
  4. Open: is reading GPL source as the spec a stated policy?We already do it with bwa; RNA-seq adds featureCounts, RSeQC and Qualimap. Recommend yes. Cell Ranger and Space Ranger stay out either way: their source is published for use with 10x products only.

Shared investments

These make several pipelines cheaper at once, so they are scheduled where their first consumer needs them.

Multi-sample runs

Tumor/normal pairs, trios and cohorts in one nomics run, with forced spill when a sample is large.

serves: Mutect2, joint genotyping, VQSR

BWA as a general aligner

Runtime scoring, -M and -C through the NEON and GPU kernels, keeping the sarek constants path and re-gating it.

serves: methylseq, ATAC-seq, ChIP-seq

An exact STAR

A second BWA-sized project: two-pass junction re-indexing, the transcriptome projection, and the thread-order RNG. the predecessor project's exact GPU seed search transfers.

serves: RNA-seq, single-cell, rnavar

New gate classes

Contents-after-untar for tar.gz outputs, decompressed comparison for gzip with timestamps, and later, if long reads are taken up, a labelled tolerance gate for neural networks.

serves: Mutect2, single-cell

Golden discipline

Pin cpus on every golden: bwa without -K and Bismark size their work by thread count, so a Nextflow retry silently changes the reference.

serves: every new family

The x86 lane

Proven today at whole-genome scale (41/41). Several new references ship x86-only containers, so their goldens are made there.

serves: long reads, Strelka-class tools

Ruled out, and why

  • Cell Ranger, Space Ranger, Cell Ranger ATAC. Source is published for use with 10x products only; reading it as a spec is legally fraught. They remain a sanity check, like GIAB.
  • Short-read DeepVariant. Cannot be exact, and Parabricks already runs it on the GPU in minutes.
  • Manta and Strelka2. Archived upstream; Strelka2 ships as an x86 CentOS 6 binary.
  • FreeBayes. 415 uses of long double, which is 80-bit on x86 and 128-bit on Arm, so even the reference disagrees with itself across platforms.
  • ASCAT, CNVkit, Control-FREEC, MSIsensor, snpEff. No port needed: run unmodified on our BAMs and VCFs, which already match sarek's, and their output matches too.
  • Bismark (default methylseq) and CUT&RUN. Both need an exact Bowtie2 first (4–6 weeks). Revisit if Bismark's Rust rewrite becomes the nf-core pin.
  • nf-core/rnafusion and raredisease as a whole. Small user base for the first; the second needs bwa-mem2 and DeepVariant before it adds anything we can make exact.
